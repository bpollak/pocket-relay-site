Setup · about 2 minutes

Get Pocket Relay working in 2 steps

Paste one command on the computer where you use Google Antigravity, then scan the QR code it shows with your phone.

Reading this on your phone? Step 1 happens on your computer. Send this page to yourself, then open it there: pocketrelay.app/setup

  1. On your computer

    Paste one command

    Use the computer where you have Google Antigravity installed and signed in.

    Mac or Linux: open Terminal and paste

    curl -fsSL https://pocketrelay.app/install | bash

    Windows: open PowerShell and paste

    irm https://pocketrelay.app/install.ps1 | iex

    This installs Pocket Relay Bridge, a small free helper that lets your phone talk to Antigravity. If Antigravity’s command-line tool is missing, it installs that too, using Google’s own installer. No administrator password, nothing outside your home folder, and it starts by itself when you log in, so you only do this once.

    When it finishes, a QR code opens in your browser. It is also in the Pocket Relay panel inside Antigravity.

  2. On your iPhone or iPad

    Scan the QR code

    Open Pocket Relay, tap Scan QR Code, and point the camera at the code on your computer. That’s it: your computer appears in the app.

    No camera handy? With your phone and computer on the same Wi‑Fi, pick your computer from the nearby list in the app and enter the 6‑digit code shown on the computer.

    Check: send a first message such as “What is in this project?” The status line changes to “Thinking”, then a reply arrives.

Good to know

  • Keep the computer on and awake. The agent runs on your computer, so a sleeping computer cannot answer.
  • Same network. Your phone reaches the computer over the same Wi‑Fi, or over a private network you run, such as Tailscale.
  • Just looking? Choose Try the demo in the app to explore it with sample data, no computer needed.

If something does not work

  • The installer says agy could not be installed: install Google Antigravity, sign in, open a new Terminal or PowerShell window, and paste the command again.
  • No QR code appeared: open http://127.0.0.1:8765/pair in a browser on the computer.
  • The app cannot reach the computer: check that the computer is awake and on the same Wi‑Fi as your phone. Guest and some office networks block this; try a home network.

Prefer to read the installer first? It is at install.sh and install.ps1. More answers are on the support page, or email brettcpollak@gmail.com. Please do not send pairing codes or passwords.
