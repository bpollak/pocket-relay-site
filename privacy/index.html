Privacy policy

What Pocket Relay does and does not do with your information.

Effective November 22, 2026

Summary. Pocket Relay has no servers of its own and no accounts. It connects only to computers you pair. The developer does not receive or store your prompts, files, approvals or voice. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Pocket Relay app for iPhone and iPad, the Pocket Relay Bridge companion that runs on your computer, this website, and support email. The developer is Brett Pollak, an individual.

How Pocket Relay works

Pocket Relay is a remote control for the coding agent on your own computer. The app talks to the Pocket Relay Bridge companion on a computer you have paired, over your local network or a private network that you run. There is no Pocket Relay server in between, so what you send and what comes back travels between your device and your computer.

Information the developer collects

None through the app or the companion. The developer does not receive your prompts, attachments, files, approvals, terminal output, voice, device identifiers or usage data. The app has no sign-in, and it contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.

The only personal information the developer receives is what you choose to send in a support email, described below.

Information stored on your device

  • Paired computers. The name and network address of each computer you pair.
  • Pairing token. A token for each paired computer, stored in the iOS Keychain. It lets the app reconnect to that computer.
  • Preferences. Settings such as appearance, and whether you have accepted the app’s AI data notice.

This information stays on your device and is not sent to the developer.

Information sent to your computer

When you use the app with a paired computer, the app sends that computer what you type or dictate, files and images you attach, your answers to the agent’s questions, and your approval decisions. Your computer sends back replies, a summary of what the agent is doing, file changes and terminal output.

The companion on your computer keeps the pairing token, settings, conversation history and its own log files on that computer. They stay on your computer and are not sent to the developer.

AI model providers

The coding agent on your computer, for example Google Antigravity, sends your prompts and the project content it reads to the AI model providers set up on that computer, such as Google. Pocket Relay does not choose those providers, does not receive what is sent to them, and does not control how they use it. Their terms and privacy policies apply. The app asks you to acknowledge this before you send your first prompt.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Local network. Used to find and connect to your computer on your Wi‑Fi.
  • Camera. Used only to scan a pairing QR code. No photos or video are stored or sent.
  • Microphone and speech recognition. Used only in Voice Mode, while you are dictating. Speech is turned into text with Apple’s speech recognition, which may process audio on Apple’s servers under Apple’s privacy policy. The developer does not receive the audio or the text.
  • Photos and files. Only the items you pick to attach are read, and they are sent to your paired computer.

You can change these at any time in the Settings app on your device.

Security

Only a device that has completed pairing with a computer can connect to it. Pairing codes expire after 10 minutes. When you connect over a private network that provides HTTPS, the connection is encrypted. A connection made directly over your local Wi‑Fi may not be encrypted, so pair and use the app on networks you trust. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Sharing and sale

The developer does not sell personal information, does not share it for advertising, and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Keeping and removing your data

To remove a paired computer and its token, delete it under Manage Hosts in the app. To remove everything the app has stored on your device, delete the app. Conversation history and logs kept by the companion stay on your computer until you remove them there. Because the developer holds none of this data, there is nothing for the developer to delete on your behalf.

Children

Pocket Relay is a tool for software developers. It is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

This website and support email

This website is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. GitHub may log a visitor’s IP address and standard request details for security under its own privacy terms.

If you email support, the developer and Google’s Gmail service receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. Support email is used only to answer you. Please do not send passwords, pairing codes or tokens.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information held about you. The developer holds no personal information from the app. For support email you have sent, write to the address below and it will be deleted on request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
