Privacy policy

What Pocket Relay does and does not do with your information.

Effective November 22, 2026

Summary. Pocket Relay has no servers of its own. It connects only to computers you pair. The developer does not receive or store your prompts, files or voice. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Pocket Relay app for iPhone and iPad and this website. The developer is Brett Pollak.

How the app connects

Pocket Relay connects only to computers you pair with it, over your local network or your own private network. The app has no servers of its own, so your conversations with your coding agent travel between your device and your computer and are not sent to the developer.

Information the developer collects

None. The developer does not receive or store your prompts, your files or your voice. The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

When you pair a computer, the app stores a pairing token for that computer in the iOS Keychain. The token lets the app reconnect to that computer.

Permissions

  • Camera. Used only to scan a pairing QR code.
  • Microphone and speech recognition. Used only in Voice Mode, for dictation. Apple’s speech recognition may process your speech; Apple’s terms and privacy policy apply to that processing.

AI model providers

The coding agent on your computer sends prompts and project content to the AI model providers configured on that computer. Pocket Relay does not choose those providers and does not control what they do. Their terms and privacy policies apply.

Removing your data

To remove a paired computer, delete it under Manage Hosts in the app. To remove everything the app has stored on your device, delete the app.

This website

This website is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. The company that hosts the site may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Independence

Pocket Relay is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Google. Google and Antigravity are trademarks of Google LLC.
