Support and setup

How to pair Pocket Relay with your computer, and what to check when it will not connect.

Get the app. Pocket Relay is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad: download Pocket Relay.

What you need

  • An iPhone or iPad running iOS or iPadOS 26 or later.
  • A Mac, Windows PC or Linux computer running Google Antigravity.
  • Pocket Relay Bridge, a small companion that runs on that computer. It can be added as a plugin inside Google Antigravity.
  • The computer must be on, awake and reachable from your device.

Pair with your computer

Scan a QR code (fastest)

  1. On your computer, open the Pocket Relay panel in Antigravity, or choose “Pair iPhone or iPad…” from the Pocket Relay Bridge menu bar icon. A pairing QR code appears.
  2. Open Pocket Relay on your iPhone or iPad and choose to connect your computer.
  3. Scan the QR code. The app asks for camera access the first time; the camera is used only for this.

Enter a 6‑digit code

  1. Connect your device and your computer to the same Wi‑Fi network.
  2. In the app, pick your computer from the list of nearby computers.
  3. Enter the 6‑digit code. Codes last 10 minutes.

Troubleshooting

The app cannot reach my computer

Check that the computer is turned on and awake. A computer that is asleep cannot be reached. Then check that Google Antigravity and Pocket Relay Bridge are running on it.

My computer is not in the nearby list

The nearby list shows computers on the same Wi‑Fi as your device. Make sure both are on the same network, or pair by scanning the QR code instead.

The code was not accepted

Pairing codes last 10 minutes. If yours has expired, pick your computer in the app again to get a new code, then enter that one.

It works at home but not elsewhere

The app has no servers of its own. It connects to your computer over your local network or your own private network, so your device must be able to reach the computer on one of those.

Remove a computer

In the app, open Manage Hosts and delete the computer. This removes that computer and its pairing from the app. To remove everything the app has stored, delete the app.

Demo mode

Pocket Relay has a built-in demo mode that lets you try the app with sample data, without a computer. In demo mode nothing runs on a computer.
